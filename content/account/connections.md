---
title: Connections
description: Link GitHub and Discord to your Robtic account.
---

Open [Settings → Connections](https://robtic.org/settings/connections).

| Connection | Used for |
|---|---|
| **Discord** | The [Discord dashboard](/discord/dashboard): it shows the servers where you're an Administrator. |
| **GitHub** | [Deploying from GitHub](/hosting/github) on Hosting, and signing in with GitHub. |

Disconnect either at any time; you can connect it again later.
