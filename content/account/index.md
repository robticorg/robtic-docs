Your Robtic account signs you in everywhere on Robtic. Create one at [robtic.org/auth/register](https://robtic.org/auth/register)
and sign in at [robtic.org/auth/login](https://robtic.org/auth/login).

## Your settings

| Page | What's there |
|---|---|
| [Profile](https://robtic.org/settings) | Your public profile. |
| [Account](https://robtic.org/settings/account) | Name, sign-in username, email, panel view. |
| [Notifications](https://robtic.org/settings/notifications) | Which emails and alerts you get. |
| [Security](/account/security) | Password, passkeys, two-factor, recovery codes, SSH keys. |
| [Connections](/account/connections) | GitHub and Discord. |
| [Devices](https://robtic.org/settings/devices) | Where you're signed in — sign out a device you don't recognise. |
| [Plan](https://robtic.org/settings/plan) | Your plan. |
