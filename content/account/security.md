Open [Settings → Security](https://robtic.org/settings/security).

## Turn on two-factor authentication

1. Press **Set up** next to **Authenticator app**.
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password…).
3. Type the 6-digit code to confirm.
4. **Save your recovery codes** somewhere safe — each one signs you in once if you lose your phone.

## Passkeys

A passkey signs you in with your fingerprint, face or device PIN — no password to type or leak. Press **Add a passkey**
and follow your browser.

## SSH keys

Add your public key to use SSH and SFTP on [Hosting](/hosting/files) without a password.

> **Tip** Check [Devices](https://robtic.org/settings/devices) now and then and sign out anything you don't recognise.
