title: Teams
description: Work together: owners, admins, managers and members.
---

Invite people to your hosting and give each a role:

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing and deleting the team. |
| **Admin** | Manage people and every workspace. |
| **Manager** | Manage the workspaces they're given. |
| **Member** | Use the workspaces they're given. |
