---
title: Workspaces
description: Group an app with its databases, backups, schedules and alerts.
---

A **workspace** holds everything that belongs together — your bot, its database, its backups — on one canvas, joined
by lines showing what uses what. Each workspace gets its share of memory, CPU and storage, which its apps share.
