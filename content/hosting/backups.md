---
title: Backups & schedules
description: Copies of your app to restore or download, by hand or on a schedule.
---

**Backups** keeps copies of your app's files. Make one before a big change, restore it if something goes wrong, or
download it. **Schedules** make backups (and restarts) automatically — every night, every week.
