---
title: Live console
description: Start, stop and restart your app, type commands and read the logs live.
---

Open an app and go to **Console**. The buttons at the top **Start**, **Stop** and **Restart** it; the console below
shows its output as it happens, and you can type commands into it.

![The live console](/shots/hosting/console.webp)

> **Note** If your app stops on its own, check the last lines of the console first — the error is usually there.
