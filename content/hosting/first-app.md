---
title: Start Hosting
description: From your code to an app that’s online, in three steps.
---

1. **Start Hosting.** Sign in to the [panel](https://robtic.org/panel) with your [Robtic account](/account) and accept Hosting's terms.
2. **Bring your code.** Press **Add** and choose: upload your files, start from a ready **Discord bot template**, or
   **clone a repository** from [GitHub](/hosting/github).
3. **Start it.** Press **Start** and watch the [live console](/hosting/console). Robtic keeps it running and
   [tells you](/hosting/alerts) when something stops.

![Adding an app](/shots/hosting/add.webp "Add: upload files, use a template, or clone from GitHub")

> **Tip** A Discord bot needs its token: add it as a [secret](/hosting/secrets) (for example `DISCORD_TOKEN`) instead of writing it in your code.
