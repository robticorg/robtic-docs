Robtic gives your server a set of bots that work together. You add the ones you need and set each of them up on
**[the dashboard](https://robtic.org/discord/public)** — no commands to memorise, every change saved in one click.

| Bot | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Robtic System](/discord/system) | Levels and XP, points, streaks, leaderboards, moderation, AutoMod, auto reply, panels and logs. |
| [Robtic Staff](/discord/staff) | Staff ranks and roles, points and promotions, applications and identity checks. |
| [Robtic Ticket](/discord/ticket) | Ticket panels, private ticket channels and transcripts. |
| [Robtic Guard](/discord/guard) | Stops scam raids: the same image posted across many channels within seconds. |
| [Robtic Voice](/discord/voice) | Music bots for your voice channels, with a control panel and text commands. |

## How it fits together

1. **Add a bot** to your server from the dashboard (you need the Administrator permission in that server).
2. **Open its dashboard** and switch on the features you want.
3. **Save.** The bot picks up the change within a few seconds — no restart.

The bots also talk to each other: Robtic System and Robtic Ticket can award [staff points](/discord/staff/points) in
Robtic Staff, and your server's overview shows statistics from every bot.
