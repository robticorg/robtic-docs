---
title: Ticket panels
description: The message members press to open a ticket.
---

A panel is a message with buttons or a menu — one option per kind of ticket (*Support*, *Report*, *Apply*…). Each
option can ask questions first and open the ticket in its own category.

1. Open **Robtic Ticket → Overview** and press **New panel**.
2. Design the message (embed or components) — click a part to edit it, press and drag a block to move it.
3. Add the **options**: name, emoji, category, questions, and who can see the tickets.
4. Pick a channel and press **Post**.

> **Tip** **Info panels** show information without opening a ticket — rules, FAQs, links.
