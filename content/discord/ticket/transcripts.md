When a ticket is closed, Robtic Ticket saves its full conversation — messages, images, who claimed it — as a
**transcript**: a web page you can open, search and share.

## Configure transcripts

Open **Robtic Ticket → Transcript**.

![Transcript settings](/shots/ticket/transcript.webp "Robtic Ticket → Transcript")

1. **Log channel** — where ticket events are logged: opened, claimed, closed.
2. **Transcript channel** — where each transcript is posted when a ticket closes.
3. **The transcript channel message** — press **Customise** to design what's posted with each transcript (text, an
   embed or components, with the file and a link button if you want them).
4. **Send the transcript to the owner** — the member who opened the ticket also gets it in a direct message.
5. **The direct message** — press **Customise** to design that message.
6. **Save changes**.

> **Note** These are the defaults. A single panel can use its own log and transcript channels — set them at the top of the panel's page.

## The archive

**Robtic Ticket → Archive** lists every closed ticket. Search by member, staff member or option, filter by date, and
open any transcript. Each transcript has a link you can share or keep private.

![Transcript archive](/shots/ticket/transcripts.webp)

## In a ticket

`/transcript` (or the **Transcript** button in the ticket) saves a transcript right away without closing the ticket.
