title: Robtic Ticket
description: Ticket panels, private ticket channels and transcripts.
---

Members open a ticket from a panel; Robtic Ticket creates a private channel for them and your support team. When it's
closed, a **transcript** is saved and can be sent to the member.

![Robtic Ticket's overview](/shots/ticket/overview.webp)

## Set it up

1. [Add Robtic Ticket](/discord/dashboard) to your server.
2. Open **Robtic Ticket → Settings**: language, support and manager roles, where transcripts go.
3. [Create a ticket panel](/discord/ticket/panels) and post it.
4. [Set up transcripts](/discord/ticket/transcripts).
5. **Save changes**.

![Ticket settings](/shots/ticket/settings.webp)

## Transcripts

Every closed ticket is kept in **Transcripts → Archive**. Search by member, staff or option, and share a transcript
with a link.

![Transcript archive](/shots/ticket/transcripts.webp)
