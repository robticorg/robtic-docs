---
title: Come, Sleep and Info panels
description: Call the owner back, close quiet tickets automatically, and show information without a ticket.
---

## Come

**Come** calls the ticket owner back with a direct message when they've gone quiet: "Staff are waiting for you in your
ticket". Use the **Come** button in the ticket or `/come`. On **Robtic Ticket → Come** you design that message.

![Come](/shots/ticket/come.webp)

## Sleep

**Sleep** handles tickets nobody answers. It reminds the owner, waits, and closes the ticket if they still don't reply.
Staff start it with the **Sleep** button or `/sleep`; on **Robtic Ticket → Sleep** you set the wait times and the
reminder.

![Sleep](/shots/ticket/sleep.webp)

## Info panels

An **info panel** shows information — rules, an FAQ, links — without opening a ticket. Build them on **Robtic Ticket →
Info panels**, then show one from a ticket option or post it in its own channel.

![Info panels](/shots/ticket/info-panels.webp)
