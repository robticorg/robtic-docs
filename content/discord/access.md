---

By default, **every Administrator** of your server can open its dashboards. You can lock them to a few people.

![Dashboard access](/shots/discord/access.webp "Server → Access")

## Lock the dashboards

1. Open your server and go to **Server → Access**.
2. Turn on **Lock the dashboards**.
3. Add the members who may still open them, then **Save**.

> **Warning** The server owner can always open the dashboards, so nobody can lock the owner out.
