Panels are messages you design on the dashboard and the bot posts: an embed or Discord's new components, with buttons
and menus that open other panels, give roles or show information.

![Panels](/shots/system/panels.webp)

## Build a panel

1. Open **Robtic System → Panels** and press **New panel**.
2. Choose **Embed** or **Components**.
3. Click any part to edit it. Use the blue **+** to add a block (text, image, buttons, a menu…).
4. **Drag a block** to move it: press it and move the mouse — the others slide out of the way.
5. Pick the channel under **Post in…** and press **Done**, then **Save changes** and **Post**.

![The panel editor](/shots/system/panel-editor.webp "Click a block to edit it, press and drag it to move it")
