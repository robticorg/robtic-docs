Robtic System is the main bot: activity rewards, moderation and the everyday tools of a community. Every part is a
**feature** you can switch on or off; most start on.

![Robtic System's overview](/shots/system/overview.webp "Robtic System → Overview: today's numbers, the last 14 days and your top members")

## Features

| Group | Pages |

| **Server** | [General](/discord/system/general) (language, prefix, roles), Emojis, Permissions, Connections |

## Turning a feature on or off

Each page has a switch in its top-right corner. Off means the bot ignores that feature completely — its commands
answer that it's off, and nothing is counted or posted. Your settings are kept for when you turn it back on.
