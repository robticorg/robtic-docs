| Page | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Partners** | Partner servers with a banner and a representative; post partnerships in one click. |
| **Referral codes** | Codes members share; each use gives a points bonus. |
| **Invites** | Who invited whom, join and leave messages, and the vanity URL. |
| **Boosts** | A thank-you message (and reward) when someone boosts. |
| **Server tag** | A role for members wearing your server tag. |
