**Robtic System → General** holds the settings every other feature relies on.

![General settings](/shots/system/general.webp)

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Language** | English or Arabic — the language of the bot's replies, embeds and buttons. |
| **Prefix** | The character before typed commands, like `!level`. Up to 5 characters; default `!`. Slash commands always work. |
| **Commands channel** | Optional: the only channel where members may use member commands. |
| **Roles** | Who counts as staff, and which roles the bot should ignore. |

## Permissions

**Robtic System → Permissions** decides who may use staff and admin commands. Members with a higher staff tier can do
everything a lower tier can.

![Permissions](/shots/system/permissions.webp)
