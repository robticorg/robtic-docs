---
title: Points & RC
description: An activity currency members earn, and RC they convert points into.
---

Points are earned from activity; **RC** is the currency members get by converting points. Nothing pays per message —
each source collects progress and pays whole points at your rate, keeping the remainder.

![Points & RC](/shots/system/points.webp)

| Source | Default |
|---|---|
| Messages | 100 messages → 1 point |
| Combo score | 100 score → 1 point |
| Voice | 10 active minutes → 1 point |
| Streaks | Your own table: days → points |
| Conversion | 100 points → 1 RC (minimum 100 points) |

## Change the rates

1. Open **Robtic System → Points & RC**.
2. Set how many messages, voice minutes and combo score make a point.
3. Set the **RC rate** and the **minimum conversion**.
4. **Save changes**.

## Commands

| Command | Who | |
|---|---|---|
| `/points balance [member]` | everyone | Points, RC and rank |
| `/points rates` | everyone | How points are earned here |
| `/points convert <points>` | everyone | Points → RC |
| `/points add · remove <member> <amount>` | admins | Grant or take points |
