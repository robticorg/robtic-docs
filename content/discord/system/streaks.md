A member keeps their streak by sending a message every day. Miss a day and it ends — unless staff give it back.

![Streaks](/shots/system/streaks.webp)

## Set it up

1. Turn on **Robtic System → Streaks**.
2. Pick the channels that count, and where milestones are announced.
3. Build the **reward table**: at *N* days, give points (up to 15 rows).
4. Optional: **reminders** before a streak expires, and the claim, expiry and return windows.
5. **Save changes**.

| Command | Who | |
|---|---|---|
| `/streak [member]` | everyone | Current streak |
| `/streak-top` | everyone | Top 5 |
| `/streak-return <member>` | staff | Give back a streak that just expired |
