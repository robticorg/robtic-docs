The dashboard lives at **[robtic.org/discord/public](https://robtic.org/discord/public)**. Sign in with your Robtic account and connect Discord;
you'll see every server where you're an **Administrator**.

![Your servers](/shots/discord/servers.webp "Pick a server: the small logos under each one are the Robtic bots it already has")

## Open a server

1. Go to [the dashboard](https://robtic.org/discord/public) and sign in.
2. Pick a server. Use the search box if you're in many.
3. The server opens on **Discussion** — the sidebar takes you everywhere else.

## The sidebar

| Section | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Discussion** | Talk with Robtic's AI about your server *(coming soon)*. |
| **Server** | **Overview** (statistics), **Activity** (every change made on the dashboards) and **Access** (who may open them). |
| **Bots** | Each Robtic bot. Open one to see its own pages; a bot that isn't in the server shows **Add**. |
| **Integration** | Connections, Plugins and Providers *(coming soon)*. |

## Overview: your server's statistics

The Overview shows one chart per statistic — messages, member joins and leaves, voice activity, staff points, tickets,
scam raids stopped… A chart only appears when its bot is in the server.

- Pick **7, 30 or 90 days** at the top. Each chart shows the total and the change against the period before.
- Hover a chart (or focus it and press **← →**) to read each day.
- Press **Customize** to arrange the page: drag charts to reorder them, switch between **line** and **bars**, make a
  chart **wide**, remove charts, or **Add chart**. **Reset** goes back to the default layout.

![Customizing the overview](/shots/discord/overview-customize.webp "Customize: drag, remove, add, line or bars, normal or wide")

> **Note** Your layout is remembered per server in your browser.

## Saving changes

Every bot page works the same way: change what you need, then press **Save changes** at the bottom. **Reset** throws
away what you haven't saved. Each saved change is listed under **Server → Activity**.

![The activity log](/shots/discord/activity.webp "Activity: who changed what, and when")
