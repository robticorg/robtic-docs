Robtic Voice runs **music bots** on your server — separate bot accounts you add with their token. A **fixed** bot stays
in one voice channel; a **roaming** bot joins whichever channel music is asked for in.

![Robtic Voice](/shots/voice/overview.webp)

## Add a music bot

1. Create a bot in the [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications) and copy its token.
2. Open **Robtic Voice → Music bots** and press **Add bot**. Paste the token, name it, and pick fixed or roaming.
3. Invite the bot to your server with the link shown.

![Music bots](/shots/voice/bots.webp)

## Turn music on or off

**Robtic Voice → General → Robtic Voice**. Off takes every music bot offline; your bots and settings are kept.

![General](/shots/voice/general.webp)

Members play music from the **control panel** (buttons) or with **text commands** in the voice channel's chat, like
`play never gonna give you up`. You choose the words for each command.
