Robtic Staff runs your staff team: a ladder of ranks, points that move people up it, breaks, applications to join, and
an identity check for new staff.

![Robtic Staff's overview](/shots/staff/overview.webp)

| Page | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Staff members** | Everyone on the team, their rank, points and status. |
| **Ranks & roles** | The ladder: which role is which rank. |
| **Points & promotion** | How points are earned, and the weekly target for promotion. |
| **Applications** | A form members fill in to apply for staff. |
| **Identity check** | Verify new staff before they get their roles. |
| **Fast Access** | Quick commands for staff. |
