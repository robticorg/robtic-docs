---
title: Set up your staff team
description: Build the rank ladder and add your staff.
---

1. [Add Robtic Staff](/discord/dashboard) to your server.
2. Open **Robtic Staff → Ranks & roles**: set the **accepted role** (every staff member gets it) and the role for each
   rank, lowest first.
3. Open **Channels** and choose where logs, applications and announcements go.
4. **Save changes**.
5. Add people to the team in Discord with `!accept @member` — or let them apply with an [application](#applications).

![Ranks & roles](/shots/staff/roles.webp)

## Promote, demote or remove

On **Staff members**, right-click someone (or press **⋯**) to promote, demote or remove them. The bot updates their
roles and logs it.

![Staff members](/shots/staff/members.webp)

## Applications

Turn on **Applications**, write the questions, and pick the channel where answers arrive for your team to review.
