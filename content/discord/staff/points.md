---
title: Staff points
description: Points for staff work — from moderation, chat and tickets — and automatic promotions.
---

Staff earn points for their work. Points come from Robtic Staff itself and from the other bots:

| From | How |
|---|---|
| **Robtic System** | A point for every few messages staff write, and points per moderation action. |
| **Robtic Ticket** | Points when a staff member claims a ticket. |
| **By hand** | Managers add or remove points. |

![Points & promotion](/shots/staff/points.webp)

Set the **weekly target** on **Points & promotion**. Every Monday the bot lists who reached it, so you can promote
them from [Staff members](/discord/staff/setup#promote-demote-or-remove).
